Critter Care Guide

A little love goes a long way in keeping your friends cozy.

🧼

Bathing

Spot clean only with a damp cloth and mild soap. If they need a deep clean, hand wash in cool water. Never use a washing machine!

☀️

Drying

Gently squeeze out excess water (don't wring!). Lay them flat on a towel in a sunny spot. Avoid the dryer at all costs—it's too scary for them!

☁️

Reshaping

If your critter looks a bit squished after transit or too many hugs, simply give them a gentle massage and a 'puff' to restore their shape.

A Note on Safety

While we use secure safety eyes, always supervise small children during playtime. These critters are best friends for those aged 3 and up!
